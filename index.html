Play Five-Card Draw

Each player gets 5 cards and 5000 free chips. Every hand opens with an ante round — the fastest click sets the entry amount, everyone else tops it up to enter or spectates that hand. Then each player may exchange 0–3 cards once (discards go to the bottom of the deck, replacements come off the top). Then one round of betting — Fold, Call, Raise or All-in — and the best 5-card hand takes the pot. No community cards, no cards in the middle.

How Five-Card Draw works

  • Goal: best 5-card hand takes the pot. Hands rank: Royal Flush > Straight Flush > Four of a Kind > Full House > Flush > Straight > Three of a Kind > Two Pair > One Pair > High Card. The wheel (A-2-3-4-5) counts as a straight.
  • Ante round (every hand): the fastest click wins — tap 10 / 100 / 500 / 1000 to set the entry amount for this hand. Everyone else must top up that amount to enter; anyone who doesn't stays a spectator for that hand and is automatically offered entry again next hand (no leaving and rejoining). If nobody enters, the round is dead — no cards are dealt, and the ante is set again (try a lower amount). A player with fewer chips than the ante goes all-in for what they have. At 0 chips you are busted and may Rebuy 5000 free chips between hands. Anyone can leave the match at any time with the Leave button.
  • Deal: 5 cards each from a single 52-card deck, dealer button rotates clockwise every hand. No community cards.
  • Draw (once per hand): on your turn, tap 0–3 cards to exchange, then confirm (0 = stand pat). Your discards go to the bottom of the deck and your replacements come off the top. Only the count is logged — never the ranks.
  • Betting (exactly one round, after all draws): first to act is left of the dealer. Call = match the bet to stay in (when nothing has been bet yet, Call reads Check). Raise = match it plus extra (minimum raise is 10 chips or the size of the last raise, whichever is bigger). All-in = bet everything you have left. Fold = give up your hand and your chips in the pot. A player who just raised can't raise again over their own raise — they must wait until somebody else raises first (they can still fold, call, check, or all-in for the call amount). Betting ends when everyone left has matched the bet, or only one player remains.
  • Chips — stage, then confirm: clicking a chip or dragging it (10 / 100 / 500 / 1000) only stages it in your bet tray — nothing leaves your stack yet. Stack as many as you like (1000 + 500 + 100 = 1600 is easy), click a staged chip to take it back, then press ✅ Bet Ready to commit. The tray tells you live what the staged bet means ("Call 200", "Raise to 700", "All-in", or what's missing). The Fold / Call / Raise / All-in buttons always work too.
  • Showdown: the last player to raise shows first, then clockwise. If everyone else folds, the survivor takes the pot without showing. Ties split the pot; any odd chip goes to the earliest winner clockwise from the dealer. Side pots are worked out properly when someone is all-in for less.
  • 🥃 Drink Whiskey: purely cosmetic, local only — it wobbles YOUR screen a little (5+ sips) or a lot (15+ sips) and never touches the game or anyone else's screen. "Sober up" clears it early.
  • Joining late? If you join a game that's already in progress, you spectate — you see the table but every card stays hidden, and you are automatically offered entry at the next hand's ante round (just stay on the screen, no leaving and rejoining). If your connection drops mid-hand, you have 10 seconds to reconnect and keep your seat; after that your seat is withdrawn and you come back as a spectator. Quit and slip back in before the 10 seconds three times in a row in the same room and you are banned from rejoining that room — a genuine dropout that stays away past 10 seconds never counts toward the three.
